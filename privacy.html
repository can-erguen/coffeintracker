4. Optional features

iCloud sync

If you turn on “Sync with iCloud”, entries, custom drinks and settings are stored via Apple’s CloudKit in the private database of your iCloud account to keep your devices in sync. This data is held by Apple under your account; we have no access to it. Apple’s privacy policy applies. You can turn this off at any time.

Apple Health

Only if you allow it, the app writes your logged drinks to Apple Health as caffeine values and reads your sleep data. Data from Apple Health is analyzed on your device only, is not stored, not transmitted to us or third parties, and never used for advertising. You can change permissions anytime in the Health app under Profile → Apps.

Apple Watch

Entries and settings are exchanged directly between iPhone and Apple Watch via Apple’s WatchConnectivity – not via our servers.

Notifications, widgets, Siri and Live Activities

Reminders (e.g. cut-off or daily limit) are scheduled locally on your device. Widgets, Shortcuts/Siri and Live Activities read data on your device. Apple’s terms for Siri apply to Siri requests.

Sharing and export

An image of your caffeine curve or a CSV export is only created when you trigger it. You decide where to share it; the terms of that service apply there.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. We do not receive payment data. The app only receives information from Apple on whether a purchase is active, to unlock features. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings. Apple is responsible for processing payments.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it.
  • Delete single entries in the app by swiping or long-pressing; delete everything under Settings → “Delete all data”.
  • Deleting the app removes local data. Delete iCloud data in the app (with iCloud sync on) or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • You can download your entries anytime via “Export as CSV”.
